# (3.4) Safeguarding Your Online Privacy

**Two Factor Authentication (2FA)** = adds an extra layer of security for account logins → besides your name and password or Personal Identification Number (PIN):
- physical object (credit card, phone, etc)
- biometric scan (face, voice, fingerprint)
- verification code sent via Short Message Service (SMS) or Email 

**Open Authorization (OAuth)** = open standard protocol that allows you to use your credentials to access third-party applications without exposing your password 
- EX: Login/Register portal provides options of logging you in using your credentials from a social media website like Facebook or via another account such as Google.
- *"You forgot your password? Login with: Facebook, LinkedIn, Google, GitHub"*

<u>Email and Web Browser Privacy:</u>
- When private mode is enabled, cookies (files saved to your device to indicate what websites you've visited) are disabled → meaning temporary internet files are removed and browsing history is deleted when closed out of the program.

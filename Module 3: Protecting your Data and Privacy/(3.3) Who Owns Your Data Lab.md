# (3.3) Who Owns Your Data Lab

**Terms of Service (Terms of Use or Terms and Conditions)** = legally binding contract that governs the rules of the relationship between you, service provider, and others who use the device.
- **Data Use Policy** = how service provider will collect, use, and share your data
- **Privacy Settings** = allow you to control who see info about you and who can access your profile or account data
- **Security Policy** = outlines what company is doing to secure the data it obtains from you

<u>Before signing up:</u>
- Have you read Terms of Service?
- What are your rights regarding your data?
- Can you request a copy of your data?
- What can the provider do with the data you uploaded?
- What happens to your data when you close your account?
